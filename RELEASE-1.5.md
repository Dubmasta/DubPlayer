The music player made for car stereos and Bluetooth speakers. Free, no account, no ads. Android 6.0 or newer.

**⬇ Download:** tap **DubPlayer-Free-1.5.apk** below, on your Android phone or tablet. Install steps are on the [project page](https://github.com/Dubmasta/DubPlayer#install-in-four-steps).

💡 **Old Android phone in a drawer?** Put your music on it and turn it into a dedicated Bluetooth music player for your car or speaker.

### New in 1.5
- **Save sound for this song** keeps every setting: Gain, Bass, Treble and the enhancement.
- **Find song info online:** "Back to matches" returns to the list you came from, including the matches found by **Identify by sound**.
- **Coming in the paid version:** **Dubmasta Sound**, one tap to make every song sound its best on big speakers. Free shows it with a small lock.
